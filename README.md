1. (Protecting Personal Savings (Stopping Scams)
Voice-Cloning Scams: Fraudsters use AI to clone a family member's voice and call asking for urgent emergency money. Detection tools in phone apps can warn users if incoming audio is synthetic.
Fake Video Calls: Scammers use deepfake faces during video calls or investment pitches. Detection tools verify whether the person on screen is a live human before money is transferred.)


2. Preventing Online Identity Theft
Identity Hijacking: If someone steals a person's photos from social media to create fake video profiles or impersonate them online, detection systems allow platforms to flag and take down the fake profiles immediately.
Securing Online Banking: When setting up a bank account or resetting a password via face verification, detection tools ensure no one can use a generated video of someone else's face to access their bank account.

3. Stopping the Spread of Fake News
Viral Video Verification: People often see shocking videos of politicians, celebrities, or news events on WhatsApp, TikTok, or X. Detection systems integrated into social media display warnings like "Manipulated Media" so users don't fall for or share fake news.

Protecting Reputation: It helps regular citizens quickly prove that a forged video or photo damaging their personal or professional reputation was synthetically created.
